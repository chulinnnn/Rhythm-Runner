# Rhythm Runner

**Rhythm Runner** is a Unity 2D rhythm-based runner. You move forward automatically through a side-scrolling world where timing matters as much as reflexes.

## About the game

Stay in sync with the music to jump, duck, and boost at the right moments. Dodge obstacles, collect coins along the run, and push your score higher the better you match the beat.

## Core gameplay

- **Rhythm-first controls** — Actions feel best when you play on the beat, not just as fast as you can mash buttons.
- **Run and react** — The character keeps moving forward; you focus on jumping, avoiding hazards, and reading what’s ahead.
- **Coins** — Pick up coins during a run to reward clean play and exploration of each route.
- **Speed up** — Use boost at the right time to surge ahead when the rhythm calls for it.
- **Obstacles** — Walls, pits, and other dangers demand quick reads and well-timed moves so a single mistake doesn’t end the run.

## What to expect

A fast, music-driven run where every section asks you to listen, react, and chain smooth moves. Easy to pick up, rewarding when you nail the rhythm.
